      Tips

        Timing calibration

        Phones and Bluetooth headphones add a small delay between the click and the mic. If the app keeps saying you're late when you feel on time, calibrate: press the button and play a short note exactly on each of the 8 clicks after the count-in. Don't use Bluetooth headphones for this.
